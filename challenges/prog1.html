∑ Sum of Primes

Some challenges don't hide anything — they just make you compute.

Task: Find the sum of all prime numbers below 1000.
The flag is CTF{that_number}.
  • A prime is divisible only by 1 and itself: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ...
  • Python: a 5-line sieve, or brute-force each number's divisors.
  • Sanity check: sum of primes below 10 = 2+3+5+7 = 17.
  • Doing this by hand is technically possible. Don't. 😄
