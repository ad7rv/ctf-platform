🤖 Ask the Robots

Every well-behaved website publishes a file that tells search-engine crawlers which parts of the site they are not allowed to index. Webmasters sometimes use it to hide their secrets... which ironically makes it the first place a hacker looks.

  • The file always sits at the root of the website.
  • It is named after the crawlers themselves: /robots.txt
  • Read ours, find the forbidden path, then visit it.
If someone tells you "don't look in the basement," where do you look first?
