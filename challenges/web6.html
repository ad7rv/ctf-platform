HR said this employee-badge form is "locked". The flag is right there, but the field refuses to cooperate. Forms are just HTML — and HTML is just a suggestion your browser chooses to follow.


  • Why is the field greyed out? Which HTML attribute causes that?
  • F12 → Elements → find the input → delete the attribute.
  • Or run one line in the console: document.querySelector('input').removeAttribute('disabled')
