🥗 Caesar's Favorite Salad

The Romans encrypted military messages by shifting every letter a fixed number of places down the alphabet. A modern variant shifts letters by exactly 13 positions — conveniently, applying it twice undoes itself.

Intercepted ciphertext:

PGS{e0g13_1fag_pelcg0}
  • Linux/Mac: echo 'PGS{e0g13_1fag_pelcg0}' | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m'
  • Or use any online "ROT13 decoder" (rot13.com, CyberChef...)
