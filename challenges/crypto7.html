🍋 The Lemon Cipher

Found on a suspect's notepad. They used a Vigenère cipher — like Caesar, but each letter shifts by a different amount based on a repeating keyword.

Ciphertext:

NXR{j1t3y3v3_o1du3c}

Scribbled in the margin: "key = my favorite sour fruit, 5 letters"

  • The keyword letters shift: A=0, B=1, ... L=11, E=4, M=12, O=14, N=13.
  • CyberChef: "Vigenère Decode" with the fruit as key.
  • Only letters were ciphered; digits/underscores passed through. Keep the original case when submitting (the decoded case IS the flag's case).
