🍪 Cookie Monster

Welcome to the bakery! While you were reading this sentence, this page baked a cookie straight into your browser.

Cookies are tiny key-value crumbs websites store in your browser. Your job: find the cookie this page just set and read what it contains.

  • Open DevTools (F12) → Application → Cookies, or
  • open the Console and type document.cookie
